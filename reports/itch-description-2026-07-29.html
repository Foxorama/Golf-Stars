Space golf, it's a roguelike RPG in space, with golf. Fly a galaxy of procedurally-generated golf courses, earn your bag, upgrade your ship, and find out what has been sleeping at the root of the World-Tree.

A whole galaxy of golf courses and competition

Fifteen worlds, each with its own physics and its own personality: links in the ice rings where the crosswind never rests, molten doglegs on the ember world, luminous spore-groves so tight you have to shape it, a star-ocean shelf with whales breaching under the cliffs. On the derelict ship you are shooting down metal corridors and caroming off bulkheads across gaps of open space.

Four ways to play

  • 🌠 Story Tour — the campaign. Five chapters, five Galaxy Tournaments, each a different format: scramble, best-ball, singles matchplay, strokeplay, and a 2v2 finale. Qualify on the road, pick your partners, and be careful when the Snake Cult comes calling.
  • 🚀 The Voyage — three arcs, three bosses, one win. Survive the cut at every stop or the tour drops you.
  • 🌌 Unending Universe — endless survival. Every hole has a number to beat. How deep can you get before the galaxy closes over you?
  • 🗺 Star Tour — free-roam the whole chart, land anywhere, play its 18, chase your own course records. Unlocked by finishing the story.

Four characters to play as

  • Feather Fade — a buttery, controlled fade on every shot. Predictable shape, tidy dispersion.
  • Huang-Woo Hook — a surgeon with the irons, but the big sticks fight a snap-hook left.
  • Longshot Larry — bombs it off the tee. Where it ends up is anyone's guess.
  • Backspin Bo — zips the short irons back on a string. Pin-seekers that bite and hold.

Hire a caddy. Regret it. Hire another.

Caddies are the run-defining pick, and taking a new one fires the one on your bag. Suggestible Sam reads the green depth and calls the club. Dr Chipinski holes out from off the green when you least deserve it. The Mystic Mole finds the line on a putt nobody else can see. Sandy Sandsaver gets you home from the beach. The Prognostic Parrot plays your shot twice and keeps the better one. They come from rival factions who each want you for something.

Earn fun cosmetics in game

Credits are for the Pro Shop this run. Star Shards are forever — spend them in the Trade Market on eighteen ships, from the station wagon you leave Earth in, to an alien mothership with fully sick spinning wheels, and over forty-plus cosmetics to outfit your team with. Ascension unlocks permanent bag tiers. A hole-in-one, in any mode, earns a ship nobody can buy.

Golf that feels like golf

Pull back to load the swing and watch the spray cone open with the power — the cone is the dispersion the game will roll against, drawn to scale, so what you see is what you're risking. Clubs launch, peak and come down at real angles: a driver runs out on firm turf, a wedge lands steep and checks. The ball skips, settles, and trickles down the fall line of a green you can read. Bad lies cost you, good gear takes it back.

Play it your way

  • Readable text — scale the whole interface, choose a legible typeface, add letter and word spacing.
  • Reduced motion — seeded from your system, and yours to override.
  • Full keyboard play — aim, modulate power and swing without a pointer, and every overlay is a proper dialog.
  • Screen-reader narration — the hole, the conditions and every shot outcome are spoken, not just drawn.
  • Aim assist on by default — the game picks a safe, sensible line and club; turn it off when you want the whole rope.

No accounts. No tracking.

The Far Carry collects nothing and sends nothing. No sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no cookies. Your save lives on your own device and never leaves it, and you can export the whole thing to a file and carry it wherever you like.

It installs as a web app and plays offline.
