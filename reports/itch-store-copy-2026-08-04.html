It's a golf RPG. You get a golfer, a bag, a ship and a cut to survive. Every course is generated when you land on it, across fifteen worlds with their own gravity, air and ways to lose a ball, so no two holes are ever the same. Hire a caddy. Buy something reckless. Fly further than the fuel really allows.

Golf that feels like golf

Pull back to load the swing and watch the spray cone open with the power. That cone is the dispersion the game will roll your shot against, drawn to scale on the map, so if it touches the water then the water is genuinely in play. Nothing is hidden behind a number you never see.

Clubs launch, peak and come down at real angles. A driver arrives shallow and runs out on firm turf. A wedge lands steep and checks. Stroke play, Stableford, matchplay, scramble and best-ball formats are implemented to keep things interesting, and Story Tour will make you prove your worth in all of them.

Three ways to play

🌠 Story Tour — the RPG

Five chapters: Qualify in two tournaments and then win a Sigil Event to earn the sigil piece and move onto the next chapter. A qualifying event is nine holes and its format is randomly determined from the game seed: stroke, stableford, or a pairs event playing scramble, best-ball or matchplay. The chapter's major opens with a strong rival to beat on the next tee.

  • You'll be able to choose whether you want to save or end the galaxy as part of the story.
  • Pick your playing partners carefully — saving the galaxy means one of your friends will turn on you.

🚀 The Voyage — the roguelike

Nine stops in three arcs: Every ordinary stop is six or seven holes against a field and you have to make the cut; miss it and the tour drops you. Each arc ends with a boss, and the last one is for victory and to increase the difficulty. You leave Earth with a full tank of gas, but travel between the stars is a fuel guzzler so make sure you top up regularly.

  • Ascension is the difficulty ladder and it goes up to fifteen. Increased difficulties unlock permanent bag tiers for every future run: the Planet set, then Phoenix Flames, then Solar Storm, which is the apex bag and blazes gold across the galaxy.
  • And every voyage you win unlocks a new start club for that golfer. So each of the four is slowly building a bag of their own.

🌌 Unending Universe — endless survival

In four hole sets: so one disaster can be clawed back by the other three. The difficulty tightens after every second set. Four over par down to four under, and then the difficulty curve is based on how hard the holes get.

Finish a Story Tour campaign and Star Tour opens up as well — free-roam the whole chart, land anywhere, play that world's designed eighteen and chase a record on every course.

A whole galaxy of golf courses

Fifteen worlds, each with its own physics and its own personality. Gravity changes the carry, heavy air drops the ball short, and the course is generated randomly every time so no two holes are exactly the same.

Links in the ice rings where the crosswind never rests. Molten doglegs on the ember world. Storms on a gas giant that will carry your drive or throw it into the next county. Cliff shelves over a star-ocean with whales breaching below. On the void world there is only the abyss. Bounce your balls off the walls in the derelict ship, the bulkheads are in play, but watch out for damaged walls where your ball can sail out and drift amongst the stars forever.

Four golfers to play as

  • Feather Fade — a buttery, controlled fade on every shot. Tightest dispersion in the game, and her left miss is all but gone.
  • Huang-Woo Hook — a surgeon with the irons and a liability with the big sticks, which fight a snap-hook left. Two different golfers depending on what you pull out.
  • Longshot Larry — bombs it off the tee and where it ends up is anyone's guess. Will not carry a hybrid. Not can't. Won't.
  • Backspin Bo — the only golfer who spins it back. Pin-seekers that land, bite and hold, paid for with distance off the tee.
  • Definitely only four golfers......

Tap a portrait on the select screen for their story — where they are from, their best win, their lowest moment.

Nine caddies to find and hire

Whoever is on the bag changes how you play.

  • Space Ducks — laser-toting ducks who zap every ball missing left back onto the short grass.
  • Convict Sheep — the right-hand mirror, with boomerangs. Between the two of them you can buy your way out of one entire side of the golf course.
  • The Prognostic Parrot — sees the shot before you play it. When he does, you get to see the shot twice before you make it and then choose to keep the better ball.
  • Dr Chipinski — holes out from off the green when you least deserve it. Calls "You rang?" as you strike it, then makes good on it.
  • The Mystic Mole — lives under the greens and has read every break there is. He aims the putt on a perfect line. You judge the pace.
  • Driver Dan — play your driver from any lie, at full power. Rough, sand, trees, doesn't matter. It is a terrible idea but with his advice you can make it work.
  • Suggestible Sam, Sandy the Sand-Saver and Penelope Putter round it out — the club call, the bad-lie rescue, and handing the whole green to somebody else.

Hiring a new one fires the one you have got, the perk walks out with them, and they will not work for you again this run. They come from rival factions, and the factions notice.

In the Story Tour you'll be able to find them as your friends to help you on your journey. They'll move into your spaceship when you hire them and you'll be able to swap between them and take on their individual quests.

45+ items for your builds and to improve the game play

Every stop has a Pro Shop and credits do not survive the run, so there is no sense saving them. Buy the Shank Guard and the red wedge on the right of your cone is gone. Not reduced. Gone. There is a grip that does the same to the duck-hook.

  • The Power Glove — Crank the pull past the top of the meter to 140% and hit the biggest bomb in the galaxy.
  • Floater Balls — buoyant, so they skip clean over water and water stops costing you a stroke. There is a heat-shielded pair for lava and an anti-grav pair that drifts across the void.
  • Grooved Driver Face — your driver can no longer be hit short. Not "shouldn't be". Can't. Commit to it or put it away.
  • Spin Trajectory Computer — draws the entire run-out before you swing. Every yard of check, every foot of curl, all the way to where the ball actually stops.
  • Full Send — a curse, and it says so. Both miss zones open up hugely, but the crowd loves a show and you get 60% more credits out of every stop.

And then there is the Rainbow Ball, whose card just says "Race the Rainbow Course at your own peril! Warning: unbalanced course. Travel here only if you are worthy…" Buy it and the hole becomes a glowing ribbon through the stars with every hazard deleted and open space either side. If you're worthy on this course, you might be lucky enough to get invited to play from The Warrior's Tee.

What you keep

Credits are for the Pro Shop, this run only. Star Shards are forever — spend them in the Trade Market on eighteen ships, from the station wagon you leave Earth in up to an alien mothership with fully sick spinning wheels, and on forty-odd cosmetics to outfit the whole team with. Buying is global and wearing is per golfer, so all four of yours can look completely different.

Explore the galaxy and find new wonders

  • ☄ The Comet Rider — a dimpled golf-ball comet blazing across the void. There is exactly one way to earn it and it takes exactly one shot.
  • 🔨 Thor's Hammer — Mjölnir, reforged as a driver, rune-etched and crackling with caught lightning. Won on the golden plains of Asgard.
  • 🔥 The Firebird — a jet-black muscle-car cruiser with a golden phoenix blazing across the hood.
  • ♾ The Infinity Ace — a phoenix-winged star-yacht wreathed in living aurora.
  • 🌳 The Trade Market — a travelling set of traders that can show up and let you buy in game items with Star Shards, or maybe just claim your ball so that they can practice.
  • 🐉 The World Serpent — Jörmungandr, ride the serpent through the stars as an unlockable spaceship. The achievement is called Beaten into Submission.

The Unending Universe and the Story Tour both have secret cosmetic unlocks, depending on how far you get.

And once, at the end, it stops being about golf

Halfway through the campaign you are asked to choose, and it cannot be undone. Hold the line and keep the World-Eater asleep, or put your clubs down and let it wake. The rest of the run bends around whichever you picked, and so does the fight you end on: Jörmungandr coiled round the root, or the Warden Ark, the Order's flagship, which will try to stop you unleashing the seal.

It is a proper ship fight. You fly, you dodge, and every weapon you bought along the way has its own trigger. Turn up under-gunned and you'll be outmatched.

Yes, that is the station wagon you left Earth in. No, it was not designed for this.

Accessibility Features

  • Readable text — scale the whole interface, choose a legible typeface, add letter and word spacing.
  • Reduced motion — seeded from your system, and yours to override.
  • Full keyboard play — aim, modulate power and swing without a pointer, and every overlay is a proper dialog.
  • Screen-reader narration — the hole, the conditions and every shot outcome are spoken, not just drawn.
  • Left-handed mode — mirrors the controls and the whole cast, so a left-handed golfer plays a left-handed game. Golf video games almost never bother. This one does.
  • Aim assist on by default — the game picks a safe, sensible line and club; turn it off when you want the whole rope.

How this game was made

The AI disclosure on this page is ticked for Code and Text & Dialog. What does that actually mean?

There are no AI-generated art assets in this game. There are no art assets at all. Every visual — the courses, the golfers, the ships, the item cards, even the icon and the banner at the top of this page — is drawn by code, as vectors and canvas drawing, while you play. Nothing came out of Midjourney or Stable Diffusion. There isn't an image file in the project.

The sound is the same. There isn't an audio file in the project either. Every note and every effect is synthesized in your browser as it plays.

Who did what:

  • The AI wrote all of the code. I wrote none of it.
  • The AI wrote most of the prose and much like this description, I reviewed it all and then edited and changed it or had it rewritten. Everything was checked and reviewed. Not all of it is good, but all of it had a human review it.
  • The design is mine. The game modes, the golf feel, the worlds, the story, the characters and the plot. I specified how it should look and sound, the AI wrote the code to make it real.
  • Some of it is the AI's own. Sometimes I'd ask for things and it'd come back different and the different was better than what I had asked for.

The working method was ask and direct for something to be implemented, then play, then reject, then refine. I would describe what I wanted, play what came back, and send it back — sometimes four or five times — because it still did not feel right. Nearly 800 commits in about five weeks, and very little of this game is a first draft.

I'll run through the entire process in more detail as a post mortem and link it here once I'm done. I still can't believe I was able to publish a playable game in 5 weeks.

Built with

No game engine. The Far Carry is hand-written TypeScript running in your browser — the play view is Canvas2D, the hole maps are SVG, and every sound is Web Audio, synthesized as it plays. It is built with Vite, tested with Vitest and headless Chromium.

The whole game ships as a single inlined index.html. No engine runtime, no asset bundle, no font files, no audio files.

No accounts. No tracking.

The Far Carry collects nothing and sends nothing. No sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no cookies. Your save lives on your own device, and you can export your save as a file to make sure it won't be lost if you clear your browser cache, uninstall or change devices.

Install it, and don't lose your save

There is nothing to download. You can install it as a proper app from farcarry.vulpecula.games — on your phone, or from the address bar on desktop — and installed it plays with no connection at all. The guide walks through it browser by browser.

Progress saves automatically on your own device. No account and nothing in the cloud, which also means there is nobody who can restore it for you — so export a backup from Settings → Save data, and do it before you ever clear your browser data. Each place you play keeps its own save: here, the website, and the installed app are three separate spaces, and the export file is the bridge between them.
